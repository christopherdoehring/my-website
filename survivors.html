What Survivors Do

In Dead by Daylight, Survivors are the four players trying to escape the Killer. Their main objective is to repair five generators, open the exit gates, and leave the Trial alive. While that sounds simple, Survivors also have to avoid being chased, rescue teammates from hooks, heal injuries, and make smart decisions under pressure.

Repair Generators

Generators are the main objective for Survivors. Repairing them requires focus, timing, and awareness because missing skill checks can alert the Killer.

Help Teammates

Survivors need teamwork to stay alive. This includes rescuing teammates from hooks, healing injured players, and protecting others during dangerous moments.

Escape the Killer

Chases are one of the most exciting parts of the game. Survivors use pallets, windows, lockers, and map knowledge to create distance and survive longer.

Why Survivors Are Fun to Play

Playing Survivor is fun because every match feels different. Sometimes the team works together perfectly, and other times everything goes wrong fast. Good Survivor gameplay requires communication, awareness, timing, and the ability to stay calm while being chased. That unpredictability is one of the reasons Dead by Daylight stays interesting after hundreds or even thousands of hours.

Dwight Fairfield

Dwight Fairfield

An unlikely leader forced to rise to the occasion, Dwight Fairfield’s reluctant bravery inspires those around him. Understanding the value of teamwork, Dwight is adept at locating and bolstering the efficiency of his teammates.

Meg Thomas

Meg Thomas

Driven by reckless courage, the athletic Meg Thomas has never been afraid to push her limits. Prone to stepping up in the clutch, Meg gets a thrill out of goading Killers into lengthy chases, buying essential time for her teammates.

Claudette Morel

Claudette Morel

Claudette understands the value of altruism, using her aptitude for botany to ensure her teammates are prepared for the challenges ahead. An expert healer to herself and others, Claudette’s supportive presence is always welcome.

Jake Park

Jake Park

A solitary specialist, Jake Park’s time in the wilderness has sharpened his survival instincts. Stealthy and resourceful, the only sign of his presence are the Sabotaged Hooks he leaves behind.

Nea Karlsson

Nea Karlsson

Years of evading authorities and navigating the streets of Sweden have kept the elusive Nea Karlsson one step ahead. Capable of escaping in a pinch, her catlike agility has gotten her out of trouble time and again.
