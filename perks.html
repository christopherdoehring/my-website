Learn about some of the most popular Survivor and Killer perks in Dead by Daylight.

What Are Perks?

Perks are special abilities that provide unique advantages during a Trial. Survivors and Killers can equip up to four perks at a time, allowing players to customize their playstyle. Some perks improve teamwork, while others help with stealth, tracking, healing, or surviving chases.

Choosing the right perks can dramatically change how a match plays out. Experienced players often build perk combinations that work together to maximize their strengths.

Popular Survivor Perks

  • Sprint Burst – Gain a burst of speed when starting to run.
  • Bond – See nearby teammates through walls.
  • Windows of Opportunity – Highlights pallets and vault locations.
  • Adrenaline – Instantly heal and gain speed when generators are completed.

Popular Killer Perks

  • No Way Out – Delays Survivors from opening exit gates.
  • Barbecue & Chili – Reveals Survivor locations after a hook.
  • Lethal Pursuer – Reveals Survivor locations at the start of the match.
  • Pain Resonance – Damages generator progress after a hook.
